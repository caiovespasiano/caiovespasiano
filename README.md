## **About**

I like building things, figuring out how they work, and turning ideas into reality.

The work spans several areas that overlap more than they look like they should:

- **3D & game development** — stylised assets, props and UGC items, plus the tooling around them.
- **Simulation** — vehicle dynamics and chassis physics in C++, and dashboards and viewers for driving and flight simulators.
- **Software & graphics** — web applications, bots, and whatever else the problem calls for.
- **UI/UX** — the part that decides whether any of the above is actually pleasant to use.

Most of it lives in private repositories. What is public here is the visible edge, not the whole picture.
